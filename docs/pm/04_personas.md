# 04. Target User Personas (Template Profiles)

> **Research Integrity Notice**: The profiles below are structured *planning templates* to guide discovery interviews. Replace placeholder assumptions with empirical interview transcripts before citing as validated research.

## Persona Template 1: Growth Product Manager
- **Representative Role**: Mid-Market SaaS PM (50–250 employees)
- **Primary Goals**: Triage weekly customer feedback in <30 minutes; defend sprint priorities with objective RICE scores.
- **Key Frustrations**: Manual spreadsheet tagging; stakeholder pushback on why pet features were deferred.
- **Core Workflows**: CSV Feedback Import, AI Insights Clusters, RICE Matrix, Roadmap Kanban.

## Persona Template 2: Senior Product Analyst
- **Representative Role**: Data & Experimentation Analyst
- **Primary Goals**: Monitor DAU/MAU, retention, and conversion trends; validate A/B tests before rollout.
- **Key Frustrations**: PMs calling underpowered tests "winners"; exporting stale CSV snapshots.
- **Core Workflows**: Product Analytics, A/B Experimentation Console, Telemetry CSV Export.

## Persona Template 3: Startup Founder / Head of Product
- **Representative Role**: Seed–Series B Founder
- **Primary Goals**: Balance roadmap velocity with unit economics (LTV:CAC, break-even, runway).
- **Key Frustrations**: Disconnected pricing spreadsheets and engineering backlogs.
- **Core Workflows**: Overview Dashboard, Business Case & ROI Modeler, PM Workspace.
