# Research Template: Customer Survey Instrument

> **Usage**: Template for quantitative user satisfaction and workflow prioritization surveys.

## Section 1: Respondent Profile
1. Primary role: `[ ] Product Manager  [ ] Product Analyst  [ ] Founder  [ ] Engineering Lead  [ ] Other`
2. Weekly feedback volume reviewed: `[ ] < 25  [ ] 25–100  [ ] 100–500  [ ] 500+`

## Section 2: Satisfaction & Friction (1–5 Likert Scale)
3. How satisfied are you with your current ability to connect customer feedback to roadmap priorities? `(1 = Very Dissatisfied, 5 = Very Satisfied)`
4. How confident are you in your team's current A/B experiment statistical reviews? `(1 = Not Confident, 5 = Very Confident)`

## Section 3: Open-Ended Diagnostics
5. What is the single biggest bottleneck in turning customer feedback into engineering tickets?
6. What report or export does leadership request most frequently that takes too long to prepare?
