# 09. Product Roadmap

## Lifecycle Stages
1. **Backlog**: Validated ideas scored via RICE awaiting sprint capacity.
2. **Planned**: Committed to an upcoming release milestone and sprint.
3. **In Progress**: Active engineering and design execution.
4. **Testing**: QA verification, staging validation, and feature-flag pilot.
5. **Released**: Shipped to production and tracked via Product Analytics.

## Milestone Schedule (Illustrative Demo Timeline)
- **v2.0 Core Launch (2026-11-15 — Released)**: Persistent filters, UTF-8 BOM CSV importer.
- **Q4 Growth Drop (2026-12-04 — Testing)**: Slack & webhook experiment significance alerts.
- **Q4 Reliability Release (2026-12-19 — In Progress)**: Async streaming export, cohort rollup caching.
- **v2.1 Enterprise Gate (2027-01-18 — Planned)**: SAML 2.0 SSO & granular RBAC.
- **Q1 Ecosystem Release (2027-02-24 — Backlog)**: Jira two-way sync, Executive PDF snapshot.
