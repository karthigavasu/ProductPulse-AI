# 02. Product Vision and Strategy

## Product Vision
Empower every product team to connect **what customers say**, **what users do**, **what engineering builds next**, and **what drives sustainable business value**—with complete statistical and analytical honesty.

## Strategic Pillars
1. **Closed-Loop Decision Traceability**: Every roadmap item should link directly to recurring customer feedback clusters, RICE scoring assumptions, and post-launch A/B validation.
2. **Explainable Intelligence Over Black-Box Hype**: Whether using the local deterministic NLP engine or Gemini 4 Hydrogen, classifications explain *why* a tag was applied and never masquerade as verified customer facts.
3. **Statistical Rigor by Default**: Built-in sample adequacy checks prevent teams from shipping false-positive experiment winners.
4. **Unit-Economics Literacy for PMs**: Product decisions are modeled alongside CAC, LTV, gross margin, and break-even thresholds.

## 12-Month Strategic Horizon
- **Phase 1 (Core Intelligence)**: Unified SQL telemetry, CSV feedback ingestion, local NLP + Gemini assistant, RICE + Kanban roadmap.
- **Phase 2 (Workflow Automation)**: Two-way Jira/Linear sync, Slack significance alerts, scheduled executive briefs.
- **Phase 3 (Enterprise Governance)**: SAML 2.0 SSO, custom cohort retention SQL builder, multi-workspace RBAC.
