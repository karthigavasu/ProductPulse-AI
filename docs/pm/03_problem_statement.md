# 03. Problem Statement

## Current State Pain Points
Product organizations at scaling B2B SaaS companies face four compounding coordination failures:

1. **Fragmented Voice of Customer**: Qualitative feedback lives in support queues and CSV dumps while quantitative usage metrics live in separate analytics tools.
2. **Opaque Prioritization Politics**: Without standardized RICE scoring and audited override rationales, roadmaps drift toward the loudest stakeholder request rather than highest customer reach and impact.
3. **Premature Experiment Conclusions**: Teams celebrate small-sample conversion lifts without checking minimum expected counts or two-tailed p-values.
4. **Disconnected Business Modeling**: Feature investments are approved without modeling subscription tier economics, churn sensitivity, or break-even customer counts.

## Desired Future State
A single, lightweight, database-backed workspace where feedback ingestion, NLP theme detection, RICE scoring, roadmap execution, A/B z-tests, and unit economics live side-by-side with clear separation between synthetic demos, modeled assumptions, and live data.
